Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Chroma Code Breaker (“the app”) is an offline single-player colour-code puzzle game.

Summary

No account is required. We do not sell personal information. Core gameplay works offline and stores progress locally on your device.

Information we collect

  • No account data (no name, email, or password required to play).
  • Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • This build does not include ads, analytics SDKs, or cloud login.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Optional native share of results uses your device’s share sheet and is controlled by you.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
